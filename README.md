QUANTUM COMPUTING CHALLENGES
#  Quantum Computing with Qiskit

## Overview
A hands-on exploration of quantum computing using Python and Qiskit, focusing on quantum circuits, gate operations, measurement, and simulation.

## Topics Covered
- Qubits, superposition, and entanglement
- Quantum gates and circuit construction
- Quantum measurement and readout errors
- Circuit simulation and measurement histograms
- Quantum noise and result analysis

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Qiskit
- Qiskit Aer
- Jupyter Notebook

## Objectives
To understand quantum principles through practical implementations and explore their potential connections to electronics, semiconductor technology, and VLSI design.

## Future Scope
Explore quantum algorithms, noise-aware circuits, and quantum circuit resource estimation.
